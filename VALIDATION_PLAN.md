# Lattice Validation Plan

## Objective

Measure whether Lattice helps the target user complete a tokenized-equity research task, recognize the thesis invalidation condition, and respect a concentration guardrail better than an unstructured chat workflow.

## Participants

- Target: 12 active retail or prosumer investors
- Capital band: USD 10,000–250,000
- Risk appetite: moderate
- Trading frequency: 2–10 trades per month
- Primary market: U.S. equities, tokenized equities, or crypto

Do not enter invented participants. Obtain consent and avoid collecting unnecessary personal information.

## Task

“You hold a simulated $40,000 AI-infrastructure basket with 35% in NVDAx. Decide whether to add to the position before the next earnings cycle, and state the evidence, risk, sizing decision, and invalidation condition.”

## Protocol

1. Record segment, capital band, and trading frequency without collecting names.
2. Ask the participant to use **Guided demo** without assistance.
3. Record completion time and whether assistance was required.
4. Record whether the participant opened an evidence receipt.
5. Ask them to state the invalidation condition from memory.
6. Ask for trust before and after using Lattice on a 1–5 scale.
7. Administer the standard 10-question System Usability Scale.
8. Capture one improvement suggestion and one confusing point.

## Metrics

| Metric | Target | Label before study |
|---|---:|---|
| Completion without assistance | ≥80% | Targeted |
| Median completion time | <300 seconds | Targeted |
| Evidence-receipt open rate | ≥50% | Targeted |
| Correct invalidation recall | ≥70% | Targeted |
| System Usability Scale | ≥75/100 | Targeted |
| Risk-guardrail bypasses | 0 | Targeted |

## Reporting rules

- Calculate results only from completed real sessions.
- Label measured results **Observed** and planned thresholds **Targeted**.
- Report sample size and exclusions.
- Do not convert usability results into investment-performance claims.
- Keep the raw anonymous CSV with the submission materials if results are cited.
